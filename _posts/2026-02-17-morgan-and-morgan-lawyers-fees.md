### Additional Costs You Might See

#### Medical Liens

A medical lien is a legal claim placed on your settlement by a healthcare provider. If you received medical treatment for your injuries and didn't pay for it out-of-pocket (e.g., used health insurance or had bills outstanding), the providers or insurers might want to be reimbursed from your settlement.

Your morgan and morgan lawyers will work to negotiate these liens down. Their goal is to maximize the money that ultimately goes into your pocket. This ensures that you aren't unfairly burdened by past medical expenses.

#### Subrogation

Similar to a medical lien, subrogation is when your health insurance company seeks reimbursement. If your insurance paid for medical treatment related to your injury, they might have a right to get that money back from your settlement. This is often outlined in your insurance policy.

Your legal team will handle these subrogation claims. They will ensure that your health insurance company only recovers what they are legally entitled to. They protect your interests during this complex process.

#### These are Separate from Morgan & Morgan's Fees

It's very important to understand that these medical liens and subrogation claims are separate from what morgan and morgan lawyers charge. They are not part of the lawyer's contingency fee. They are third-party claims on the money you win.
