#### No Upfront Fees – The Contingency Basis

Another crucial way the "for the people slogan" comes to life is through their fee structure. Many people avoid legal help because they worry about expensive hourly rates. Morgan & Morgan operates on a contingency fee basis, which means you pay nothing upfront.

This is a huge relief for someone who is already dealing with medical bills and lost income due to an injury. You don't have to pay any legal fees out of your own pocket while your case is ongoing. It means financial worries won't stop you from getting the justice you deserve.

Instead, morgan and morgan lawyers only get paid if they win your case. If they secure a settlement or a verdict in your favor, their fee comes as a percentage of that recovery. If they don't win, you don't owe them for their legal services. This setup truly aligns their success with yours.

This model is a powerful statement of their "for the people slogan," showing they believe in your case enough to invest their own time and resources. It ensures that quality legal representation is available to everyone, regardless of their current financial situation. It makes legal help accessible.
